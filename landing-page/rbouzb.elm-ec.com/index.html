    SECTOR 05 — QUERIES

    Before you download.

    Is it really free?

    Yes — free to download. The listing says it offers in-app purchases for some items and functions, and it names a monthly subscription, so expect optional paid extras rather than a paywall on the install.

    What kind of game is it?

    A real-time strategy game themed on wolves. You assemble a pack, defend your den, explore a shared world map and fight other packs, with alliances carrying the co-operative side.

    Do I have to play with other people?

    Alliances, PVP and cross-server play are central to the listing's description, so the game is built for other players to be present. Treat it as an online multiplayer strategy game.

    Can I play offline?

    No. The listing states a network connection is required to play.

    Which devices does it run on?

    The listing asks for iOS 15.0 or later. Check the App Store page for the current requirement before you install.
